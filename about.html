Established 2005

Connecting ASEAN to the World

The Conceptine Group was incorporated in Malaysia in 2005 as a systems integrator. Over the years, we have consistently grown from a single regional office into a standard technology footprint with active presences in Cambodia, Myanmar, and Singapore.

Our core operations cover key data center and telecommunications infrastructure in addition to our legacy systems integration offerings. The company continues to deliver to the most exacting standards in the enterprise technology industry.
